About Craftora

Connecting artisans, craft lovers and communities while preserving the beauty of traditional handicrafts.

WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Craftora

Craftora is a web-based handicraft development and community management platform created to connect talented artisans with customers, learners and craft communities.

Our platform provides artisans with a digital space to showcase their handmade products, share their skills and participate in workshops, exhibitions and competitions.

At the same time, customers and craft lovers can discover unique handmade creations and learn more about the people and traditional skills behind them.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support local artisans, preserve the beauty of traditional handicrafts, and create meaningful opportunities for artisans to share their skills and handmade products with a wider community through technology.

OUR COMMUNITY

Artisans • Customers • Learners

Craftora is built around a community where artisans can share their creativity and skills, customers can discover and support handmade products, and learners can gain knowledge from experienced craft makers.

By bringing these groups together in one platform, Craftora aims to create meaningful connections and new opportunities within the handicraft community.
